How to collect money (quick version)

  1. Create a Razorpay account (razorpay.com) and complete KYC — best for India (UPI, cards, netbanking).
  2. Make a Payment Link for Pro Monthly and Pro Lifetime — no coding needed to start.
  3. Paste the link in Pricing & payments, turn off demo checkout, and share the app.
  4. When someone pays, generate a license key in the License keys tab and email it to them.
  5. For automation: add a small server + Razorpay webhook that verifies the payment and issues keys automatically.
